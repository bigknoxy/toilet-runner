Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 16, 2026

Overview

Toilet Runner is a free, offline-capable game. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data Collection

Toilet Runner does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no analytics, no tracking, no advertisements, and no third-party SDKs.

Local Storage

The game saves your scores, settings, and unlocked skins locally on your device using your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Internet Access

The game loads Google Fonts for display purposes. Once cached, the game works fully offline. No user data is sent over the network.

Children's Privacy

The game does not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.
